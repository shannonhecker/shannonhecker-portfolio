Privacy.

This notice covers optional analytics, the Ask Shannon chat and the game scoreboard.

Ask Shannon chat

When you send a message to Ask Shannon, the message is sent to my private chat API and then to Anthropic so the assistant can respond. The chat is for questions about my work, projects, approach, and availability. Please do not include confidential, sensitive, or client information.

I may review chat messages and responses to improve the portfolio and spot abuse. Chat logs are kept for up to 30 days by default. The chat service stores the conversation text, technical browser information, and a pseudonymous visitor ID. Third-party IP geolocation is off by default.

Optional analytics

Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity load only when you choose “Allow analytics”. Google Analytics measures visits and reading depth. Advertising features are off. I exclude nicknames, form entries and query strings from the page views and scroll events I send. Clarity records clicks and scrolling for heatmaps and session replays. The scoreboard and chat are masked in recordings. Clarity can capture query strings in page addresses.

Your choice lasts six months on this browser. Withdrawing consent stops both tools and clears their site cookies. The page reloads if the tools were already running. I cannot clear cookies Microsoft sets on its own domains.

The game scoreboard

Completed stages and mission stars stay in your browser. Clearing site data resets your progress. Sharing a score is optional. If you choose to share, your score and optional nickname appear on the public stage scoreboard. Please choose a made-up name.

Vercel and Upstash process scoreboard data. Each stage stores up to 100 results and shows the best ten. Run receipts expire after two hours. To limit spam, the scoreboard stores a keyed address digest for one hour. Its database does not store raw addresses.

To remove a score, contact me with the stage, nickname and score.

Contact

If you email me, I use your email and any information you include to reply. I do not sell personal information.

Fonts

Pages that use Google Fonts request their typefaces from Google. Your browser sends technical information, including its IP address, when it makes that request.

Your choices

  • You can use the direct email link instead of the AI chat.
  • You can avoid including personal or confidential information in chat messages.
  • You can ask me to delete a chat or email conversation by contacting shannonheckerchen@gmail.com.
