Fusion Data Solution

Enterprise data workflows turned into guided journeys. 40+ screens, five reusable frameworks, WCAG AA.

about.

Overview

Designed the interface for an enterprise data management platform, turning dense technical workflows into guided experiences for data analysts and operations teams.

The platform handled large-scale ingestion, validation, and distribution across business lines. Users needed to monitor data quality, configure automated rules, and resolve exceptions quickly.

My Role

As Product Designer, I owned the design from stakeholder workshops and user research through to detailed Figma specifications and developer handoff.

I focused on configuration screens, error states, and consistency across core modules, balancing power-user needs with onboarding clarity.

Key Focus Areas

Data quality monitoring, workflow automation, transaction management, and reporting dashboards. All used progressive disclosure so they worked for both new and expert users.

The main challenge was balancing information density with clarity, especially in tables, filters, and rule-builder flows.
