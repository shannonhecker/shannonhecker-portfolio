Fusion Analytics Dashboard

Performance analytics dashboard for trading and ops teams. Built on Fusion Data Cubes: fast data selection, clear visualisations, reusable patterns.

about.

Project Overview

I worked on Fusion’s performance analytics features, surfacing returns, attribution, and risk across asset classes. We partnered with FactSet to integrate their analytics engine into Fusion’s reporting interface.

The challenge was turning complex analytics into board-ready dashboards people could act on. The interface had to support advanced filtering and deep slicing of data, stay readable under pressure, and move the legacy RaaS UI into a responsive web layout.

My Role

As Product Designer and UI Lead, I drove most day-to-day design decisions across the year: tokens, layout, component patterns, and accessibility. I defined the component structure in Figma against Salt Design System, turning trading desk needs into reusable patterns across the module.

Design Process

I worked with the product owner on requirements, then iterated with UX, product, development, and an ADA specialist through review cycles.

The dashboard had to handle large real-time and historical datasets, with filters, toggles, aggregation views, and comparison tools. I kept the interface modular so it could adapt to different data cube complexity and user permissions.
